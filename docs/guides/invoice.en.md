<!-- section:summary -->
## 1 · What this process is

The supplier's claim to be paid, and Paragon's decision on it. One document per claim, whichever side is looking at it. The invoice is ONE canonical record in the portal: the supplier page (`/supplier/invoices`, "My Invoices") and the buyer page (`/buyer/invoices`, "Invoices & Payment") both read the same row and show it through their own status labels, so the two sides can never disagree about where a bill stands.

Two roles touch it. The supplier's **back-office contact** (lane `back_office`) drafts the invoice against one of its own confirmed purchase orders and then submits it. Paragon's **finance officer** (lane `finance`) approves it, releases the payment, and may dispute it or resolve a dispute. Nobody presses the 3-way match: it is computed by the platform when a goods receipt on the same purchase order is posted, when an invoice is submitted, and when a dispute is resolved. Nobody presses the remittance step either: the money arriving is a bank fact, not a portal act.

It starts when the supplier creates a draft and ends at **Remittance Received**, the only terminal state. Between them the document passes through Submitted, Matched, Approved, the SAP interim **Releasing Payment**, and **Payment Released**. **Disputed** is the exception path, reachable from Submitted, Matched and Approved, and it leads back to Submitted.

Honesty markers a reader needs before using this guide. The release of payment crosses an SAP boundary: the portal act is real and recorded, but the SAP settlement callback that mints the FI document is **SIMULATED** in this build; no payment leaves any bank. The remittance step (`t_invoice_remit`) is authored and owned by the bank/SAP settlement feed; there is **no feed** connected, so no invoice in this build ever reaches Remittance Received. The demo invoices are **SIMULATED** fixtures, shifted onto the declared present (31 Aug 2026) so the aging and overdue reading stays coherent. Approval and payment release are recorded against a named person and store who decided (`approvedBy`, `releasedBy`): a seat that names nobody is refused both, and the person who approved may not release. The two seeded Approved invoices name no approver, so their payment is not released until a named person approves them again (**Approve again**). An invoice raised from the form states its lines — material, quantity, the order's unit price — and its amount is their total; lines that claim more than was received and accepted on the order are refused (`invoice_lines_within_received`). Whether the invoice then matches is still decided by the 3-way match. Seeded invoices carry no lines.

<!-- src: src/services/transitions/flows/invoice.flow.ts:34-210; src/services/transitions/cascades.ts:36-38 -->

**Forks**

- **At Submitted:** `t_invoice_match` — automation — when the match runs (a receipt posting on the same PO, the invoice being submitted, or a dispute being resolved) and the verdict is `Matched`; `t_invoice_dispute` — finance — when Paragon will not take the claim as it stands.
- **At Matched:** `t_invoice_approve` — finance — when the claim is owed; `t_invoice_dispute` — finance — when a variance or credit-note question stands in the way.
- **At Approved:** `t_invoice_release_payment` — finance — when the payment instruction is to go to SAP; `t_invoice_dispute` — finance — when the decision to pay is withdrawn before money moves.
- **At Disputed:** one exit only, `t_invoice_resolve` — finance — when the disagreement is settled; the claim returns to Submitted for re-match.

## 3 · Step by step

### t_invoice_create — Create a draft invoice <!-- transition:t_invoice_create -->

- **Step kind:** operator action (creation)
- **Role:** supplier · back_office (atom `invoice:submit`)
- **From → to:** ∅ → Draft
- **Operator — where:** `/supplier/invoices` → header button **New invoice** → panel "New invoice" → **Create draft**. If your seat does not hold `invoice:submit`, the button's slot shows "Awaiting Supplier Back Office" instead.
- **Operator — do:** The supplier starts a bill against an order it has served, while the figures are still theirs to correct. Pick the purchase order from the "Purchase order" list (only your own POs in state Confirmed are offered; if there are none the list says "No confirmed POs available to invoice."). Choosing an order opens its lines under "Lines to invoice": each line shows the material, the "Order unit price" and the quantity "Received and accepted", with one quantity field opened on that accepted quantity. Leave it to invoice everything received, or lower it to invoice less (type 0 to leave a line off the invoice); a quantity above the accepted one is refused. "Invoice amount" is the total of the lines and is not typed. Press Create draft. The portal assigns the invoice number (form `INV-2026-9001`, `INV-2026-9002`…) and shows "INV-… drafted · Draft created against PO-… · cmd_… recorded."
- **Operator — fill:** the purchase order reference (required by the machine: `poReference`); one quantity per order line (digits only; a blank, a non-number or an ambiguous "1.500" is refused with its own message, and so is a quantity above the one received and accepted). The form sends `amount` — the lines' total — and `lines`, one row per order line (`materialCode`, `qty`, `unitPrice` at the order's price); a line left at zero is sent as zero, not dropped.
- **Tester — expected state:** Draft
- **Tester — confirm:** the new row appears at the top of the supplier's list with status pill Draft and a **Submit** button; the buyer's Invoice Queue does NOT show it (the buyer read filters Draft out). The panel's key facts show "SAP FI doc — pending —" and "Payment ref — pending —", and a section "Invoiced lines" lists each line as material · quantity × unit price = line total; the buyer's drawer shows the same section once the invoice is submitted. Specimen: on a `sup-002` seat choose PO-2025-00102 — the form opens on RM-EMUL-9410 with "Order unit price" Rp 109.375, "Received and accepted" 8,000 KG and "Invoice amount" Rp 875.000.000.
- **Tester — trigger event:** `t_invoice_create`
- **Checks that can refuse:** `invoice_create_po_confirmed` — the parent PO must exist and be in state Confirmed ("PO … is not Confirmed" / "parent PO not found"). Then `invoice_lines_within_received`, which examines a payload that states `lines` and refuses, with nothing created: `INVOICE_LINES_MALFORMED` ("lines must be a list of { materialCode, qty, unitPrice } with a quantity and a price that are numbers of zero or more", or "a material is stated on more than one line"); `INVOICE_LINE_NOT_ON_ORDER` ("… is not a line of PO-…"); `INVOICE_LINE_PRICE_NOT_ORDER_PRICE` ("… is invoiced at a price other than the order's (…). An invoice is raised at the order's price"); `INVOICE_LINE_EXCEEDS_RECEIVED` ("… of … is invoiced and … was received and accepted on PO-…. Invoice that quantity or less"); `INVOICE_NOTHING_INVOICED` ("no line invoices a quantity above zero"); `INVOICE_AMOUNT_NOT_LINES_TOTAL` ("the amount (…) is not the total of the lines (…)"). The lines are read in the order stated and the first refusal is the one reported. A payload that states no `lines` is not examined by this hook; its amount is judged by the match. Before any dispatch the form holds **Create draft** and says why: under a quantity field, "Enter the quantity to invoice — a blank is not a quantity of zero. Type 0 to leave this line off the invoice.", "That is not a quantity — type digits only, e.g. 4500.", "This can be read two ways — "1.500" means one thousand five hundred in Indonesian and one-point-five in English. Type it without separators: 1500." or "More than was received and accepted on this line (… KG). Invoice that quantity or less."; with every line at zero, "Every line is at zero. Invoice a quantity on at least one line."; and on an order with no receipt posting or posted, in place of the lines, "Nothing has been received and accepted on this order yet, so there is nothing to invoice. An invoice is raised for what Paragon has received." Scope — a supplier may draft only against its OWN PO; another supplier's PO is refused `SCOPE_DENIED` before any other check.
- **Glossary:** `MISSING_FIELDS`, `POLICY_REJECTED`, `SCOPE_DENIED`, `EMPTY_QTY`, `NOT_NUMERIC`, `AMBIGUOUS_QTY`.
- **Honesty:** Dates are defaulted by the store, not the wall clock: submitted date = the declared present, due date = +30 days (Net 30), so a fresh draft is never born overdue. The new invoice starts with match status `Pending`. The SAP FI document and payment reference stay empty until settlement. What was "received and accepted" is read from the receipts on the order that are posting or posted to SAP — the supplier's own, the same receipts and the same per-line allocation the 3-way match uses; a receipt that is inspected but not posted counts for nothing. So an invoice cannot be drafted from the form before a receipt on the order is posting or posted. The form and the dispatcher ask one predicate, so the six lines refusals are reachable only by a hand-crafted dispatch; a hand-crafted `t_invoice_create` that states no `lines` is still admitted with any amount, and stores none. The lines check does not replace the match: an invoice admitted line by line can still come out not Matched. On PO-2025-00102 the seeded `inv-mus-0214` is for the same Rp 875.000.000 on the same order and is older, so when the match runs it is taken first, and a new invoice for the full 8,000 KG is held as Qty Mismatch. A seeded invoice carries no lines and shows no "Invoiced lines" section.

### t_invoice_submit — Submit the invoice <!-- transition:t_invoice_submit -->

- **Step kind:** operator action
- **Role:** supplier · back_office (atom `invoice:submit`)
- **From → to:** Draft → Submitted
- **Operator — where:** `/supplier/invoices` → the Draft row → **Submit** (in the Action column). A seat without the atom sees "Awaiting Supplier Back Office" in that slot.
- **Operator — do:** The supplier formally asks to be paid; the clock on payment terms starts running here. Press Submit on the draft. The toast reads "INV-… submitted for approval · cmd_… recorded. Awaiting 3-way match and approval."
- **Operator — fill:** nothing new to type — the row's amount is sent as the required `amount`.
- **Tester — expected state:** Submitted
- **Tester — confirm:** supplier pill changes from Draft to **Pending Approval** (the supplier label for Submitted and Matched); the buyer's Invoice Queue now lists it with label **Pending Match** and a "3-way match" column reading Pending; the buyer drawer footer says "Review match". That is the reading when no receipt on the PO is posting or posted (an invoice drafted from the form always has one, because the form drafts only against an order with a receipt posting or posted). If one is, the match runs at the submission itself: the row already carries a verdict with its figures, and on a `Matched` verdict the invoice is already Matched (footer **Approve for payment**).
- **Tester — trigger event:** `t_invoice_submit`
- **Checks that can refuse:** `invoice_amount_is_lines_total` — on an invoice that carries lines, a stated `amount` that is not the lines' total is refused (`INVOICE_AMOUNT_NOT_LINES_TOTAL`: "the amount (…) is not the total of this invoice's lines (…). An invoice that states its lines is submitted for their total"); the invoice stays Draft, as it was. An invoice that carries no lines is not examined and submits the amount it states.
- **Glossary:** `ILLEGAL_TRANSITION`, `MISSING_FIELDS`, `ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED`, `POLICY_REJECTED`.
- **Honesty:** **Submit** always sends the amount stored on the row, which on an invoice with lines is their total, so `INVOICE_AMOUNT_NOT_LINES_TOTAL` is reachable only by a hand-crafted dispatch. The check is there so that the lines check at creation cannot be undone by submitting a different amount. Pressing "Review match" on the buyer side changes nothing: the toast says "Awaiting 3-way match — Nothing was changed here. The match runs by itself when a receipt is posted on the purchase order and when an invoice is submitted or returned from a dispute." The match is a cascade, not a button.

### t_invoice_match — 3-way match (computed) <!-- transition:t_invoice_match -->

- **Step kind:** cascade (system-driven); fired by `t_gr_post`, `t_invoice_submit` and `t_invoice_resolve`
- **Role:** automation (atom `invoice:match`; no human lane holds it)
- **From → to:** Submitted → Matched
- **Operator — where:** nobody presses this in the portal. The match runs on three events: a receipt being posted at `/buyer/goods-receipt` (the GR inspection wizard, **Post to SAP**), an invoice being submitted, and a dispute being resolved.
- **Operator — do:** Paragon checks the bill against what was ordered, what was received and what has already been invoiced. Each time the match runs, every Submitted invoice on that PO gets its verdict recomputed from all receipts on the PO that are posting or posted (Posting to SAP, Posted to SAP) and all invoices on the PO. An invoice is **Matched** only while its amount fits inside what was received and accepted on the PO, less what earlier invoices on that PO already claim. Three figures, all at the PO's own unit prices: *ordered* = Σ confirmed qty × unit price; *received* = Σ accepted qty × unit price over those receipts, accepted quantity pooled per material and each PO line capped at its confirmed quantity; *already invoiced* = the sum of the other invoices on the PO in Matched, Approved, Releasing Payment, Payment Released or Remittance Received. The tolerance is 1% of the ordered value. Four causes: within what is payable → **Matched**; more than was received and accepted → **Qty Mismatch**; would fit what was received, but earlier invoices already claim it → **Qty Mismatch**; above the whole order at PO prices → **Price Variance** (the only case shown as a price variance — the match reads the invoice's total and never its lines, so it cannot say which price or quantity differs). Rejected quantity is not payable, but a receipt with rejects no longer forces a mismatch: an invoice for the accepted part matches. An invoice for less than what is payable matches (partial invoices are allowed). Invoices awaiting a match are taken oldest first (submitted date, then invoice number), and one that matches claims its amount before the next is looked at, so of two invoices for the same goods the first is matched and the second is held. Only a `Matched` verdict advances the invoice header; any other verdict is written and the invoice stays Submitted (an honest no-op, not a defect). With no posting or posted receipt on the PO nothing is written and the invoice stays Pending.
- **Operator — fill:** nothing to fill.
- **Tester — expected state:** Matched (buyer label stays **Pending Match**; supplier label stays **Pending Approval**)
- **Tester — confirm:** buyer drawer "3-way match" section reads "The invoice is within what was received and accepted on the purchase order, after earlier invoices." and shows four figures — "Ordered (confirmed quantity × PO price)", "Received and accepted", "Already invoiced", "This invoice"; the footer's primary slot now says **Approve for payment**. Use `inv-fir-0325` (INV-2026-FIR-0325, PO-2025-00104, 540,000,000) with receipt `GR-2026-012` (Approved; the PO's own line FR-MKOV-5510, 200 accepted, 0 rejected) — the one seeded pair whose verdict is `Matched`. When the PO's stated total differs from the sum of its own lines by more than 1%, the same section adds "The purchase order states a total of …, but its lines come to …; the match uses the lines." A seeded row that carries no figures reads "Recorded as matched. This row carries no match figures." (or "Recorded as a quantity mismatch. …" / "Recorded as a price variance. …"); Pending reads "Not matched yet: no receipt is posted on this purchase order."
- **Tester — trigger event:** `t_invoice_match` (its event carries `causationId` = the correlationId of the command that caused it: the GR post, the invoice submission or the dispute resolution)
- **Checks that can refuse:** `invoice_rollup_matched` — the header may advance only when the invoice's own match status is `Matched` ("match axis is 'Qty Mismatch', not 'Matched'"). The cascade resolver never fires the verb on a variance, so this hook only bites a hand-crafted dispatch.
- **Glossary:** `POLICY_REJECTED`; match statuses Pending, Pending GR, Matched, Qty Mismatch, Price Variance.
- **Honesty:** The match runs from both sides: a receipt posting finds its invoices, and an invoice submitted after its receipt posted is matched by its own submission. The verdict is recomputed from every posting or posted receipt and every invoice on the PO each time, so a held invoice is cleared by a further receipt and a resolved dispute is matched again. The verdict is stored with the figures it rests on (`matchBasis`). The match reads the invoice's amount; the lines an invoice states are not read by it, and the price-variance sentence on screen says so. A verdict other than `Matched` writes no event. A receipt that is inspected but not posted (Approved / Partially Approved) is not read. An invoice whose amount is not a positive number is given no verdict and counts for nothing as already invoiced. The 1% tolerance is a provisional placeholder, not the AP policy. `Pending GR` appears on seeded rows only — a runtime-created invoice shows `Pending` until a receipt posts, because the sub-flow verb that would write `Pending GR` is unwired.

### t_invoice_approve — Approve for payment <!-- transition:t_invoice_approve -->

- **Step kind:** operator action
- **Role:** buyer · finance (atom `invoice:approve`)
- **From → to:** Matched → Approved
- **Operator — where:** `/buyer/invoices` → Invoice Queue → open the invoice → footer primary button **Approve for payment**. A procurement seat sees "Awaiting Finance" in that slot instead of the button.
- **Operator — do:** Finance agrees the claim is owed; the decision to pay is taken here, the money moves later. Press Approve for payment. - **Operator — fill:** nothing to fill.
- **Tester — expected state:** Approved
- **Tester — confirm:** buyer pill reads **Approved** (or **Overdue** if the due date has passed); the footer now offers **Release payment**; supplier pill reads **Approved**; the "Approved" filter chip and the "Pending Approval" KPI on the buyer page move. - **Tester — trigger event:** `t_invoice_approve`
- **Checks that can refuse:** `invoice_approver_named` — the seat must name a person (`INVOICE_APPROVER_UNATTRIBUTED`); the invoice stays Matched and nothing is stamped. The reader sees "This seat names no person, and an approval is recorded against the person who decided it. Choose a sample user on the identity panel, then approve again."
- **Glossary:** `ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED`, `ILLEGAL_TRANSITION`, `POLICY_REJECTED`, `NO_PERSON_IN_SESSION`.
- **Honesty:** No seeded invoice sits in Matched, so this button is reachable only after the cascade above has run. ### t_invoice_reapprove — Approve again <!-- transition:t_invoice_reapprove -->

- **Step kind:** records a fact (state-preserving) · operator action
- **Role:** buyer · finance (atom `invoice:approve`)
- **From → to:** Approved → same state
- **Operator — where:** `/buyer/invoices` → Invoice Queue → open an Approved invoice whose approval names nobody → the drawer reads "No named person approved this invoice. A named person approves it again before its payment is released." under **Approver** → footer primary button **Approve again** (it stands where **Release payment** would). A procurement seat sees "Awaiting Finance" in that slot.
- **Operator — do:** put a name to an approval that has none. The invoice was approved — it is in Approved — but nobody answers for the decision, so its money is not released. A named finance person takes the approval again; the invoice does not move, and the release becomes available to a different named person.
- **Operator — fill:** nothing to fill.
- **Tester — expected state:** Approved (unchanged)
- **Tester — confirm:** toast "INV-… — approved for payment · Approved by {person}. The payment is released by somebody else."; the drawer's **Approver** field shows the recorded person and the warning line is gone; the footer now offers **Release payment**.
- **Tester — trigger event:** `t_invoice_reapprove`
- **Checks that can refuse:** `invoice_approver_named` — the seat must name a person (`INVOICE_APPROVER_UNATTRIBUTED`), with the sentence shown under `t_invoice_approve`. `invoice_reapproval_owed` — an approval that already names a person is not overwritten (`INVOICE_ALREADY_APPROVED`): "This invoice already has a named approver. A second approval would overwrite who decided it."
- **Glossary:** `ROLE_NOT_PERMITTED`, `ILLEGAL_TRANSITION`, `POLICY_REJECTED`, `NO_PERSON_IN_SESSION`.
- **Honesty:** the verb exists for the two seeded Approved invoices (and any approval recorded before the rule): a fixture row was approved by nobody the portal can name. The page offers **Approve again** only while the approval names nobody, so the second refusal is reachable only by a hand-crafted dispatch. The person who approves again may not then release the payment.

### t_invoice_release_payment — Release payment (SAP boundary) <!-- transition:t_invoice_release_payment -->

- **Step kind:** operator action · SAP boundary (settles)
- **Role:** buyer · finance (atom `invoice:pay`)
- **From → to:** Approved → Releasing Payment ⇒ Payment Released (on settlement)
- **Operator — where:** `/buyer/invoices` → open an Approved invoice → footer **Release payment** → confirm panel "Confirm payment release" → **Confirm release — Rp …**.
- **Operator — do:** Sends the payment instruction to SAP; until SAP confirms, nobody should tell a supplier they have been paid. When the invoice carries a bank account the confirm panel warns "This action cannot be undone. Payment of … will be transferred to … Verify bank details before confirming." An invoice raised in the portal carries none; the panel then reads "This action cannot be undone. Payment of … will be released. The supplier’s bank account is not held in the portal — SAP pays to the account on the vendor record." and the Payment section shows "Not held in the portal". Either way the panel states that the settlement callback is simulated. After confirming, the first toast says "releasing payment — Submitted to SAP for payment — awaiting the FI-document callback. No payment posted yet."; about 1.2 seconds later the settle lands and a second toast says "payment released — SAP assigned the FI document on settlement."
- **Operator — fill:** nothing to fill; the confirmation click is the act.
- **Tester — expected state:** Releasing Payment immediately (buyer label **Payment Released**, supplier label **Approved**), then Payment Released after settlement (both labels **Payment Released**)
- **Tester — confirm:** while in flight the footer reads "Awaiting SAP settlement — no FI document yet". After settlement the drawer's "SAP documents" shows a minted FI document (`FI-5100011001`…) and a payment reference (`PAY-2026-95001`…) with payment date = the declared present, and the key facts show "Payment released by" with the recorded person (or "Not recorded — no person in session"); the supplier row shows a **Payment details** action and the panel offers "View payment details". ## 4 · Decision forks and exception paths

- **Match or dispute (at Submitted).** Branch A — `t_invoice_match` — **When:** the match runs (a receipt posting on the same PO, the invoice being submitted, or a dispute being resolved) and the verdict is `Matched`; the platform fires it, nobody presses it. Branch B — `t_invoice_dispute` — **When:** finance will not take the claim (a Qty Mismatch or Price Variance verdict was written, a credit note is needed, the figures are wrong). Such a verdict on its own leaves the invoice in Submitted with the verdict and its figures visible; the human choice is then dispute, or wait for a further receipt on the same PO — the match runs again when it posts. An invoice admitted line by line at creation can still be held here: the lines check holds each line to what was received and accepted, and the match also subtracts what earlier invoices on the order already claim.
- **Approve or dispute (at Matched).** Branch A — `t_invoice_approve` — **When:** the claim is owed. Branch B — `t_invoice_dispute` — **When:** something outside the 3-way check stands in the way.
- **Release or dispute (at Approved).** Branch A — `t_invoice_release_payment` — **When:** the payment instruction is to go to SAP; this is the one irreversible commit on the buyer surface and it takes a confirmation step. A named person who approved the invoice is refused here (`INVOICE_RELEASER_IS_APPROVER`), and so is a seat that names nobody once the approval is named (`INVOICE_RELEASER_UNNAMED`); the invoice stays Approved until a different named person releases it. Branch B — `t_invoice_dispute` — **When:** the decision to pay is withdrawn before money moves. An Approved invoice whose due date has passed shows the label **Overdue** but keeps both branches.
- **Dispute → resolve (at Disputed).** `t_invoice_resolve` — **When:** the disagreement is settled; the only exit, and it returns the bill to Submitted for re-checking rather than to where it was.
- **Settlement wait (at Releasing Payment).** No verb is legal here. **When** the settle fails with a `TRANSPORT` fault the footer offers **Retry settlement**; **when** it fails `REFUSED` or `UNGOVERNED` the footer says asking again will not change the answer and the invoice stays until the refusal is resolved.
