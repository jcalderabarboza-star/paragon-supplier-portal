**What this is:** a requirements list, not a readiness claim. It is the specification for **SE-2** (hosting in AWS Jakarta) and **SE-13** (security and operations), and an input to SE-3, SE-4 and SE-8. Every row says what the tree specifies, what it already does, and where it says nothing. The tree holds no personal data today: every store is an in-memory fixture, every supplier is fictional (`src/data/mockSuppliers.ts`), sample people are role-named rows, and the actor is `UNATTRIBUTED: NO_PERSON_IN_SESSION` unless a sample person is opted into. UU PDP obligations attach when real people's data does (C12 §6.5).

## 4 · Authentication, authorisation, sessions — SE-3, SE-13

| Requirement | What the tree does today | What the SE Team builds |
|---|---|---|
| Access to the pre-release deployment | SEC-GATE-01: Vercel routing middleware (`middleware.js`) before the SPA rewrite; HMAC-SHA256 signed, HttpOnly, 7-day cookie; credentials from non-`VITE_` env (`GATE_USER`, `GATE_PASSWORD`, `GATE_SECRET`, documented in `.env.example` since #375); fails closed (503); noindex; logic in `gate/`; gate suite floor 7 | replace with real sign-in; keep noindex until launch |
| Sign-in | none. `src/pages/auth/Login.tsx` picks a persona; it collects no email and no password (A1, H3) | OIDC for Paragon staff (Entra); a supplier IdP (D-ID-2); the IdP supplies a `SubjectBinding` and nothing else (C11 V16) |
| Authorisation | atom-based, no fallback (C11 V3); module gate first (`MODULE_INACTIVE`), then role, then tenancy on every read and write (C11 V1, `describeScopingConformance`); refusal is not an existence oracle (V2). *New since v2:* one read is gated on role — the Super Admin activity view, for the Super Admin and Compliance only (`maySeeSuperAdminActivity`; it fires no transition, so it asks role ids, not an atom); and the master-data read has its own tenancy: a buyer reads everything, a supplier its own supplier record and the reference tables, another supplier's key answers `null` exactly as an absent key does, and materials, where-used and the sync log are refused `SCOPE_DENIED` for the whole object (C13 §6). *At the re-pin (#439):* a master-data change request is a buyer document — its target names no owner, so a supplier scope is refused at scope on every act; four atoms gate it (`masterdata:propose`: procurement, planning; `masterdata:review`: planning, compliance; `masterdata:handoff` and `masterdata:record-sap-answer`: procurement), and a hook holds a reviewer to its own kind (`MDR_WRONG_LANE`: materials are planning's, suppliers compliance's; a superset role — `admin`, `super_admin`, `buyer_all` — passes it) | enforce the same server-side; run both conformance factories against the real service; a role-gated page must be gated on the server, not only in the navigation |
| Decisions that need a named person | PR approval, publication signing, module switching, delivery acts, and since v1 the accept or dispute of a supplier's answer, publishing / cancelling / awarding / advancing / concluding a sourcing event, scoring a proposal, and (since #411–#417) invoice approval, the supplier, certificate, preferred-supplier, material-request, role and stock decisions, requisition approve and reject, forecast publish, discard and withdraw, and the three goods-receipt dispositions refuse an unattributed seat; a sample person may not switch modules in production (`MODULE_SET_NOT_SAMPLE_IN_PROD`). *New since v2:* creating a goods receipt (ADM-1), every act a person takes on a process change — creating and saving a draft included — and both step acts (Flow Builder) refuse an unattributed seat; a sample person may not approve, activate or restore a process change on production (`FLOWVERSION_SAMPLE_IN_PRODUCTION`). *At the re-pin (#439):* every act of a master-data change request — all nine verbs — refuses an unattributed seat (`MDR_ACTOR_UNATTRIBUTED`; `sup1NamedSeat.test.ts` holds the list both ways). **That flow has no production bar for a sample person:** a named sample person may propose, review, agree and send a request on any deployment, where the plan had recommended a bar (D6 §2; backlog row 181). What it refuses on production is the stand-in for SAP: a sample answer (`MDR_SAMPLE_ANSWER_IN_PRODUCTION`) and the sample sync (`SAMPLE_SYNC_IN_PRODUCTION`) | real persons make these real; keep the sample-person locks for non-production |
| Segregation of duties | mutual where declared (V4); two open questions (D6): one seat revising and approving a requisition; one seat allocating, signing and publishing a forecast publication. Since OPS-1 a named person who approved an invoice is refused its release. **v2 said here:** *"an approval by an unnamed seat is not guarded (D6)"*; that was already false at v2's pin (refused since OPS-2, #411). *New since v2:* whoever proposed a process change may not approve it, and a step may require a different person from an earlier step's completer. *At the re-pin (#439):* whoever proposed a master-data request may not approve or reject it (`MDR_APPROVER_IS_PROPOSER`). The v3 census (D7 §6.4) reads the whole catalogue for this: eight verbs carry a separation hook, and fifteen approve / reject / grant / award / verify / activate verbs name who decided without checking that the decider is a different person from whoever raised the document; whether each should is an open decision for Paragon (D6 §2), not a defect by itself. **One seat is exempt:** a named `super_admin` passes nine checks (the heads named in the `exempt?.(` calls in `policies.ts`; eight before #439): the same-person checks on invoice release, a preferred-supplier decision, a material-request decision, a process-change approval and a master-data request decision; the two step separations; the preferred-supplier seat check; and the sample-identity lock on loosening an enforcement or delivery-policy setting. Each time it gives a one-line reason; without one the act is refused by name (`SUPER_ADMIN_REASON_REQUIRED`), and with one the reason is recorded on the event (`bypass`) and on the document where there is one; `admin` holds the same permissions and is not exempt; the exemption belongs to the role id, so a custom role copied from `super_admin` does not carry it; a sample Super Admin is not exempt on production (PR #420; C1) | enforce once persons exist; decide who may hold `super_admin` and how its use is reviewed — the activity view is the only review surface and it reads an in-memory trail |
| Session state | `localStorage['paragon.identity']` holds the seat. *New since v2:* `localStorage['paragon.flowVersions']` holds the process changes, including the one in force — a control whose only copy is in one browser. `localStorage['paragon.customRoles']` likewise holds custom role definitions in the browser (D4 §5 lists all nine keys). The step ledger is in memory only (`src/services/flowBuilder/stepLedger.ts`), and so are master-data change requests (`masterDataRequestStore.ts`) | server-issued session; never trust client-held roles; never trust a client-held process version |
| What a supplier may read of a competitor | since SRC-2 a supplier's read of a sourcing event is an allowlist projection (`src/services/data/rfqSupplierView.ts`): no other supplier's id, no scores, no knock-out answers | enforce the same projection server-side; an allowlist, not a blocklist |
| What leaves the portal toward SAP (new at the re-pin, #439) | **Nothing leaves today.** The two settlement verbs settle against a mock, and a master-data request's hand-off (`t_mdr_hand_off`) builds a package and stops: SAP is not connected and no call is made. The package is what the real call would carry: the request number (SAP's external reference), the kind, the object and subject key, the proposed fields, the free-text reason, the `personId` of who proposed and of who agreed, and the base the request was written against (C13 §8). **Simulated, and marked:** on a demo or preview deployment a person may record a sample answer, which delivers a `SAMPLE`-stamped record into the replica through the sync applier, and may run one prepared sample sync; both are refused on production. An answer not marked as a sample is refused (`MDR_SAP_NOT_CONNECTED`) | when the call to SAP MDG is built (SE-6): decide whether a portal `personId` and a free-text reason may cross to SAP, and under which identifier; authenticate the call and the answer feed; the answer feed acts under the automation grant with the request number as its idempotency key, so it must not be reachable by a person's session |
| Secrets | none in the bundle; `VITE_*` is public by construction (`VITE_CHAOS*`, `VITE_QA_HARNESS`, dev-gated) | a secret manager; rotate `GATE_SECRET` at transfer; the AG Grid licence key (SE-1) is injected at build from a non-`VITE_` variable and is not a secret, but it does not live in the repository |

## 8 · What this document does not claim

It does not claim UU PDP compliance, a threat model, penetration testing, or encryption at rest or in transit (no code or document in the tree mentions encryption). Those are SE-Team deliverables that start from this list.
